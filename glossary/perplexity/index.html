Perplexity

A measure of how surprised a model is by text it has not seen; lower means better next-token prediction.

Perplexity is the exponential of the average loss a language model incurs predicting each token of a held-out text. A perplexity of 1 would mean perfect prediction. Because it is measured the same way across model generations and does not saturate like a benchmark, OpenAI's chief scientist proposed in November 2026 that labs use pretraining perplexity as a shared gauge of capability and agree not to train past a threshold until monitoring improves. Comparisons across labs are complicated by different tokenisers and evaluation corpora, so published figures need methodology attached.
