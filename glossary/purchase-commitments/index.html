Purchase commitments

Contracts a company has signed to buy components, capacity or services in future, disclosed in its financial statements even though the cash has not yet been spent.

For AI hardware companies they show how much supply has been locked in; for AI labs they show how much compute has been promised. Nvidia disclosed $390 billion of purchase commitments in November 2026, up from $279 billion four months earlier, and Anthropic's IPO filing showed about $900 billion of committed spending, mostly on compute. If demand falls short the commitments become inventory write-downs or penalty payments, which is why analysts read them as a measure of risk as much as of confidence.
