Nov 25, 2026 · DevelopingThe FCA says some personal AI agents will need financial authorisation at launch, publishes its agentic AI expectations, and reminds firms that accountability stays with the regulated firm and named senior managers

The guidance, published Tuesday, tells technology providers to test their products against six regulated activities: payment services, financial promotions, authorised information services, credit broking, intermediation and advice. It says the boundary is set by Parliament, not the regulator, and that senior managers answer for AI models in their area whether built in-house or bought. Firms are told to consider how they identify an AI agent, what consents it holds, what it may do and whether to accept payments from agent-controlled wallets. A dedicated Agentic Policy Sprint is promised with no date set; four UK digital regulators wrote to ministers on November 16 warning that several personal agents were about to launch.

The work, published Monday with the partner Onava, reports 18 hits against IL-17A, 8 against VEGF-A, 5 against PD-L1, 3 against IL-7Rα and 1 against TrkA, plus 179 of 272 therapeutic antibody fragments binding PD-1 or PD-L1. Nov 24, 2026 · Google DeepMind uses teams of Gemini 4 agents and Lean 4 proofs to find quantum algorithms with formally verified correctness, cutting the computing cost of a fusion simulation 11.8x and a state-preparation routine 290x

Each problem got an independent team of agents whose proposals were checked in Lean before entering a shared notebook, with teams exchanging ideas every four hours; every result is a proved Hoare triple with resource bounds, checked by an independent comparator against frozen specifications, because the circuits cannot be tested classically or on current hardware. The blog reports the inertial-confinement-fusion computation falling from 5,650 to 4,338 error-corrected qubits, a photochemistry problem 8x cheaper and a cytochrome P450 simulation 2.5x, with the Lean library and solutions open-sourced; the optimised circuits still exceed what the first error-corrected machines will offer.

BeginnersThe AI did not run a quantum computer; it found shorter recipes and proved, mathematically, that they are correct.
