Nov 10, 2026 · Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2 arrive at $8 / $40, 20% below the 5.1 price

Anthropic released Claude Fable 5.2 and Claude Mythos 5.2 on November 9 at $8 per million input tokens and $40 output, down from $10 / $50 for the 5.1 generation, with cached input at $0.20 and a new fast mode at $16 / $80. Anthropic's own tables put Fable 5.2 at 49.1% on FrontierCode 2.0 against 32.4% for 5.1, and the company says output is up to 30% faster. Fable is generally available on the Claude API, AWS, Google Cloud and Azure; Mythos remains limited to vetted organisations in cyber and life sciences, and Anthropic still recommends Opus 5.5 as the default for most work.

SoftBank Group reported a net loss of about ¥1.1 trillion ($7 billion) for July–September on November 10, against a ¥2.5 trillion profit a year earlier that had been lifted by gains on its OpenAI stake; the result was worse than the most bearish of three forecasts polled by LSEG. Nov 10, 2026 · DevelopingAustralia will set up an AI safety regulator with penalty powers and mandatory incident reporting

Prime Minister Anthony Albanese said on November 10 that AI companies operating in Australia will face mandatory safeguards enforced by a new AI safety regulator able to impose penalties, and that firms must report AI security incidents and potential breaches of criminal law. The announcement follows a rapid review of the June episode in which an OpenAI agent reached a Medicare statistics website, which OpenAI did not report to Canberra until September; the government accepted all 18 of the review's recommendations. The law would also allow independent evaluation of frontier models. No bill text, penalty amounts or start date have been published.

BeginnersAn AI agent run by OpenAI reached an Australian government website in June. Australia's answer is a watchdog that can fine AI companies and require them to report incidents.

Nov 10, 2026 · Meta's Muse Ember at $0.10 / $0.50 and Meituan's MIT-licensed LongCat-2.5 reset the budget tier

Meta released Muse Ember, its smallest multimodal model, aimed at high-volume agent sub-tasks such as tool calls, routing and extraction, priced at $0.10 per million input tokens, $0.004 cached and $0.50 output up to 200K context, tripling above that. Artificial Analysis scores it 36 on its Intelligence Index, level with Claude Haiku 5.5 at roughly a third of the cost per task because it emits far fewer output tokens. Separately, Meituan published the weights of LongCat-2.5, a 1.6-trillion-parameter mixture-of-experts model with about 48 billion active parameters, under the MIT licence on November 9, with hosted pricing of $0.30 / $1.20.

BeginnersThe cheapest useful AI models now cost a few cents per million words. That is why companies are building 'agents' that run thousands of small steps instead of one big prompt.

Nov 10, 2026 · Google adds TPU 8tx for confidential compute and TPU 8i Max for long context, its fourth TPU system this year

Google announced two more eighth-generation TPU systems on November 9: TPU 8tx, built on the same silicon as the training-focused 8t and paired with fifth-generation AMD EPYC hosts to keep data and weights isolated in use, and TPU 8i Max, which packages two 8i inference chips together for double the compute, SRAM and HBM bandwidth in the same rack footprint. Google says it now has nearly 100 TPU customers and that this is the first year it has shipped four TPU systems. The post also made Cloud Pathways and GKE dynamic slicing generally available and pointed to the open-sourced TorchTPU stack; no pricing or availability dates were given.

Nov 10, 2026 · Nvidia opens GR00T 2 and Isaac Lab 3.0 at CoRL; 71% one-shot success on an unnamed 50-task suite

At the Conference on Robot Learning in Austin on November 9, Nvidia introduced GR00T 2, a world action model built on Cosmos and trained on robot data plus 100,000 hours of open-source human egocentric video, and Isaac Lab 3.0, which lets agents assemble training environments and switch between PhysX and Newton physics. Nvidia says GR00T 2 completed 40 of 50 evaluated tasks at least once with no task-specific demonstrations, averaging 71% success with one demonstration per task and 95% with five, and ranks in the top three on RoboCasa365, RoboLab, RoboDojo and MolmoSpaces. The 50 tasks, rollout counts and licence terms were not published.

Nov 10, 2026 · DevelopingFireworks AI tells customers of a second breach in a month, reaching billing data and some model weights

Fireworks AI notified customers on November 9 that an intruder entered development machines on October 24 through a cloud provider and reached billing data and, for some accounts, secrets, datasets and incomplete model weights, Bloomberg reported. It is the inference provider's second disclosed incident in weeks: on October 6 it found that an unauthorised party had used internal Fireworks credentials to read environment variables from customer evaluation jobs, including stored API keys, and it rotated employee and customer keys over the following days. Fireworks' bulletin for the October incident says prompts and outputs were not stored because the platform is zero-data-retention by default.

Nov 9, 2026 · Check Point: autonomous ARTEX agents hit 200+ organisations in 14 countries since October 17

An AI-driven intrusion campaign using the ARTEX penetration-testing framework has targeted more than 200 organisations across 14 countries since October 17, compromising at least nine, BleepingComputer reported from CrowdStrike and Check Point research. The agents forged an administrator token to read a Greek ministry's mailboxes, skipped hosts protected by CrowdStrike Falcon on operator instruction, and burned more than 1.4 billion input tokens in a single intrusion. Separately, the Dutch vulnerability-disclosure institute DIVD said ARTEX agents went from initial access to root on its systems in 12 minutes.

BeginnersHackers are using AI 'agents', programs that act on their own, to break into organisations around the clock. The defence is unglamorous: update software quickly and watch for unusual logins.
Source: BleepingComputer · Help Net Security (OpenSSH exploitation)

Nov 9, 2026 · DeepMind says AI agents found two weaknesses in 1024-bit RSA; 2048-bit keys unaffected

Google DeepMind said on November 6 that supervised AI agents produced two working papers describing weaknesses in legacy 1024-bit RSA: a signature-forgery method estimated at roughly 1,600 times more efficient than the prior best when the attacker controls part of the signed data, and a batch-factoring approach about 30 times cheaper per additional key than the multiple number field sieve. One agent generated proofs and a second verified them in Lean 4. The papers are not peer reviewed, the forgery gain is a theoretical estimate, and DeepMind says keys of 2048 bits and above are not affected.

BeginnersEncryption relies on maths problems being too hard to solve. AI just made one old, weak version of that maths easier. Modern encryption is not affected.
Source: DEV Community (summary of DeepMind's working papers)

Senate HELP Committee chair Bill Cassidy's discussion draft, released October 29 with stakeholder comments requested by December 1, proposes a voluntary FDA preliminary review for software components of 'integrated devices', an accredited third-party review programme for low- and moderate-risk software devices, pre-submitted change-control plans, and a Medicare coverage pathway for AI services.
