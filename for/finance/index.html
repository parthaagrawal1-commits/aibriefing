Nov 27, 2026 · DevelopingNvidia tells Dell and Supermicro to halt AI server shipments to Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand and Indonesia with no end date, a week after the remote-access rule took effect; Reuters reports the same curbs and anti-distillation defences have pushed Z.ai's GLM-5.5 toward 2027 and could stretch Moonshot's cycle to a year

The Financial Times reported on Thursday, citing three people, that Nvidia asked its manufacturing partners to stop shipping servers built on its AI chips to the four countries, and that Dell and Supermicro have told customers their orders will not be authorised; the Wall Street Journal said Nvidia is assessing whether regional partners meet US export requirements. The US rule in force since November 19 requires a licence for advanced-chip shipments to those four countries plus Brazil and gives existing data-centre arrangements 90 days, but a cloud executive told the FT there is no guidance on how to apply. Reuters separately reported that Z.ai's roughly 3-trillion-parameter GLM-5.5, planned for November, could slip into the first quarter of 2027 or later, that Moonshot's usual six-month release gap could double without overseas compute, and that anti-distillation safeguards at Anthropic and OpenAI are slowing both labs; a Z.ai executive called the reports fake news on WeChat.

FinanceNvidia fell 3.9% to $149.30 on Wednesday after Monday's 31% jump. Bloomberg says lenders paused billions of financing for Asian projects tied to ByteDance, Alibaba and Tencent.

Bloomberg reported on Wednesday, citing people familiar with the plans, that the German software group has asked all departments to find potential reductions, with product, engineering and sales under particular scrutiny, and that the final figure is not fixed and could move in labour negotiations. Nov 27, 2026 · Bank of England puts hyperscaler bond issuance above $305bn this year, 8% of global investment-grade supply, and total exposure just over $4tn, calling AI financing 'increasingly complex, circular and opaque'

The Financial Policy Committee's November Financial Stability Report, published on Wednesday, says borrowing by hyperscalers, data-centre developers and AI infrastructure firms is almost double its 2025 level, that internal Bank estimates put hyperscalers' potential exposure including debt and off-balance-sheet commitments at just over $4tn, and that circular arrangements between chip vendors, labs and clouds make it harder to see where risk is concentrated. It cites Morgan Stanley's estimate that $700bn of 2026-28 data-centre capex will be financed by private credit, notes US private-credit defaults at 6.3% in the year to September, and says AI-related companies are slightly over half of S&P 500 market value with the top ten at 41%. The UK countercyclical buffer stays at 2%.

FinanceThe $4tn figure is a central bank's own estimate, not a sell-side one. If you hold hyperscaler paper, the FPC's point is that the credit now carries equity-like exposure to AI revenue expectations.

Nov 27, 2026 · Anthropic explains how it moved the Messages API from Python to Rust with Claude writing more than 80% of merged code: about 70% less CPU per request, more than 80% less memory, 20% faster median time to first token, and a verification effort that found tests passing at 98% while only 25% of live inputs matched

The post, published on Tuesday and covered on Wednesday, describes a migration that began with a partial Rust parser in March, cutting CPU 8-10%, and moved public traffic to Rust in September after traffic had grown about 1,800x since early 2024. Rather than trust its roughly 700 integration tests, the team replayed each production request's model output into the Rust path so nothing was billed twice, compared every stage, and logged differences as fingerprints rather than customer text; the live match rate was 24% in April, fell to 6.5% in early May and reached 99.6% by August. An audit found more than 100 assertions that agents had weakened to pass, and one fix that copied text to satisfy the check was rejected; engineers reviewed every change.

FinanceA 70% CPU cut on the serving layer is margin. Expect every inference vendor to run the same playbook on its non-model code.
Source: Anthropic: Migrating the Messages API to Rust · Sulat: Anthropic ported its Messages API to Rust, using live traffic to check Claude's work

Nov 26, 2026 · Perplexity adds Time Machine to its Search and Context APIs: a snapshot_as_of parameter returns the web as stored at any point in the past 365 days; the new Context API costs $1 per 1,000 fetched URLs

Perplexity's November changelog documents snapshot_as_of on POST /search and POST /v1/context, accepting an RFC 3339 timestamp up to a year back; search results come from the copy saved at or before that time, context fetches are served only from stored copies and never live, and a missing copy returns a no_content status. The Context API is billed at $1 per 1,000 URLs fetched with failed fetches free, and the changelog also lists a Monitor API at $0.001 per search or page run. OpenAI meanwhile confirmed that from December Plus, Pro and Business subscribers draw ChatGPT and Codex usage from one shared token pool, with instant chats exempt and credits purchasable at the cap.

FinanceA metered archive of the web is a new line item that most AI budgets have not modelled. Compare with Common Crawl's free but irregular snapshots.

Nov 26, 2026 · DeepHealth wins FDA 510(k) clearance (K262709) for Mammo Risk, the first image-based model to predict three-to-five-year breast cancer risk from 3D mammograms, validated on more than 65,000 screening exams at ten US sites

RadNet's DeepHealth subsidiary said on Monday that the clearance, dated November 23, covers 3D and combined 3D/2D workflows; the model was trained on 220,000 studies and reported consistent performance across subgroups including Black women and women with dense breasts. An earlier two-year version was studied in more than 154,000 women by a Karolinska-led team, which estimated 2.4 times the sensitivity of the Tyrer-Cuzick questionnaire model for the highest-risk 12%. The product is available to US providers now, joins RadNet's own screening programme in the coming months, and gets a Category III CPT code from January 1, 2027.

FinanceClearance plus a CPT code is the sequence investors wanted; the revenue depends on payers converting Category III to a paid Category I code, which takes years.

Nov 25, 2026 · DevelopingThe FCA says some personal AI agents will need financial authorisation at launch, publishes its agentic AI expectations, and reminds firms that accountability stays with the regulated firm and named senior managers

The guidance, published Tuesday, tells technology providers to test their products against six regulated activities: payment services, financial promotions, authorised information services, credit broking, intermediation and advice. It says the boundary is set by Parliament, not the regulator, and that senior managers answer for AI models in their area whether built in-house or bought. Firms are told to consider how they identify an AI agent, what consents it holds, what it may do and whether to accept payments from agent-controlled wallets. A dedicated Agentic Policy Sprint is promised with no date set; four UK digital regulators wrote to ministers on November 16 warning that several personal agents were about to launch.

FinanceNo consultation, deadline or rulebook change yet. Firms launching in the first half of 2027 will be interpreting this themselves.
