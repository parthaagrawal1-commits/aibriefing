Nov 26, 2026 · Neuralink receives FDA permission for its first pivotal trial of the LINK implant, in people with ALS, after more than 30 participants in feasibility studies

Neuralink said on Tuesday night that the FDA had approved the pivotal study of its N1 implant and R1 surgical robot for a limited number of adults with amyotrophic lateral sclerosis, with endpoints built around controlling digital devices by thought and functional outcomes that improve daily independence, alongside long-term safety and reliability. The company did not disclose enrolment size or eligibility beyond ALS; the approval builds on more than 30 implanted participants across the US, Canada and the UK. A pivotal trial is the evidence stage that precedes a request for market authorisation.

FinanceTrial size, follow-up length and the number of sites decide the burn before any revenue. None are public yet.

Nov 26, 2026 · Perplexity adds Time Machine to its Search and Context APIs: a snapshot_as_of parameter returns the web as stored at any point in the past 365 days; the new Context API costs $1 per 1,000 fetched URLs

Perplexity's November changelog documents snapshot_as_of on POST /search and POST /v1/context, accepting an RFC 3339 timestamp up to a year back; search results come from the copy saved at or before that time, context fetches are served only from stored copies and never live, and a missing copy returns a no_content status. The Context API is billed at $1 per 1,000 URLs fetched with failed fetches free, and the changelog also lists a Monitor API at $0.001 per search or page run. OpenAI meanwhile confirmed that from December Plus, Pro and Business subscribers draw ChatGPT and Codex usage from one shared token pool, with instant chats exempt and credits purchasable at the cap.

FinanceA metered archive of the web is a new line item that most AI budgets have not modelled. Compare with Common Crawl's free but irregular snapshots.

Nov 26, 2026 · DeepHealth wins FDA 510(k) clearance (K262709) for Mammo Risk, the first image-based model to predict three-to-five-year breast cancer risk from 3D mammograms, validated on more than 65,000 screening exams at ten US sites

RadNet's DeepHealth subsidiary said on Monday that the clearance, dated November 23, covers 3D and combined 3D/2D workflows; the model was trained on 220,000 studies and reported consistent performance across subgroups including Black women and women with dense breasts. An earlier two-year version was studied in more than 154,000 women by a Karolinska-led team, which estimated 2.4 times the sensitivity of the Tyrer-Cuzick questionnaire model for the highest-risk 12%. The product is available to US providers now, joins RadNet's own screening programme in the coming months, and gets a Category III CPT code from January 1, 2027.

FinanceClearance plus a CPT code is the sequence investors wanted; the revenue depends on payers converting Category III to a paid Category I code, which takes years.

Nov 25, 2026 · DevelopingThe FCA says some personal AI agents will need financial authorisation at launch, publishes its agentic AI expectations, and reminds firms that accountability stays with the regulated firm and named senior managers

The guidance, published Tuesday, tells technology providers to test their products against six regulated activities: payment services, financial promotions, authorised information services, credit broking, intermediation and advice. It says the boundary is set by Parliament, not the regulator, and that senior managers answer for AI models in their area whether built in-house or bought. Firms are told to consider how they identify an AI agent, what consents it holds, what it may do and whether to accept payments from agent-controlled wallets. A dedicated Agentic Policy Sprint is promised with no date set; four UK digital regulators wrote to ministers on November 16 warning that several personal agents were about to launch.

FinanceNo consultation, deadline or rulebook change yet. Firms launching in the first half of 2027 will be interpreting this themselves.

The work, published Monday with the partner Onava, reports 18 hits against IL-17A, 8 against VEGF-A, 5 against PD-L1, 3 against IL-7Rα and 1 against TrkA, plus 179 of 272 therapeutic antibody fragments binding PD-1 or PD-L1. Nov 24, 2026 · Z.ai releases GLM-5.4, a roughly 750B 'Loop Transformer' that runs a 28-layer block twice per token, with image input and a 1M context, to Coding Plan subscribers only: no benchmarks, no API price and no weights yet

Z.ai converted the trained GLM-5 base through continued training rather than pretraining a looped model, with per-token gates controlling the second pass and the pass reusing the first pass's KV cache; it claims capability comparable to models of two to three trillion parameters but has published only a 30B ablation showing eight to eleven points of gain on maths and science for about 10% more compute.
