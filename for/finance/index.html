Nov 25, 2026 · DevelopingThe FCA says some personal AI agents will need financial authorisation at launch, publishes its agentic AI expectations, and reminds firms that accountability stays with the regulated firm and named senior managers

The guidance, published Tuesday, tells technology providers to test their products against six regulated activities: payment services, financial promotions, authorised information services, credit broking, intermediation and advice. It says the boundary is set by Parliament, not the regulator, and that senior managers answer for AI models in their area whether built in-house or bought. Firms are told to consider how they identify an AI agent, what consents it holds, what it may do and whether to accept payments from agent-controlled wallets. A dedicated Agentic Policy Sprint is promised with no date set; four UK digital regulators wrote to ministers on November 16 warning that several personal agents were about to launch.

FinanceNo consultation, deadline or rulebook change yet. Firms launching in the first half of 2027 will be interpreting this themselves.

Nov 25, 2026 · OpenAI reports wet-lab binding for proteins designed by GPT-6 with no biology-specific architecture: 35 of 322 minibinders bound across five targets, a 10.9% hit rate, and 128 of 182 antibody loop redesigns kept binding

The work, published Monday with the partner Onava, reports 18 hits against IL-17A, 8 against VEGF-A, 5 against PD-L1, 3 against IL-7Rα and 1 against TrkA, plus 179 of 272 therapeutic antibody fragments binding PD-1 or PD-L1. DockQ scores with side chains hidden rise from about 0.05 for GPT-5.6 to 0.57 for GPT-6.2 and 0.81 for an unnamed internal model. OpenAI says RFdiffusion's published minibinder hit rates were higher, that results depend on filtering, and that this does not demonstrate end-to-end de novo design; on full structure prediction the internal model averages a TM-score of 0.38 against ESMFold2's 0.93.

FinanceTreat any 'beats the specialists' framing as unsupported by this release, and note the strongest numbers come from a model nobody outside OpenAI can test.

Nov 24, 2026 · Anthropic launches Claude Ship in beta: PR Steward fixes failing CI and review feedback, Claude Approvals applies a plain-language policy file and can approve pull requests, and a /ship command runs the whole flow from Claude Code; at least $10,000 of credits for Enterprise

The bundle pairs the generally available Code Review with two beta agents for GitHub repositories on Claude Team and Enterprise: PR Steward resolves merge conflicts, fixes checks and comments after each push with a rollback path but cannot approve or merge, while Approvals reads a policy file from the default branch and can auto-approve routine changes or escalate to named people, with GitHub branch protection still applying. Nov 24, 2026 · Z.ai releases GLM-5.4, a roughly 750B 'Loop Transformer' that runs a 28-layer block twice per token, with image input and a 1M context, to Coding Plan subscribers only: no benchmarks, no API price and no weights yet

Z.ai converted the trained GLM-5 base through continued training rather than pretraining a looped model, with per-token gates controlling the second pass and the pass reusing the first pass's KV cache; it claims capability comparable to models of two to three trillion parameters but has published only a 30B ablation showing eight to eleven points of gain on maths and science for about 10% more compute. The release follows Thinking Machines' Inkling 2 Mini, which loops its middle layers three times to fit in 20 GB. An API is promised after safety evaluation and weights after hardening, without dates; GLM-5.3 stays the API model at $1.40 / $4.40.

FinanceLooping spends FLOPs to save memory, which is the constrained input; whether it is passed through in price is the open question.

Nov 23, 2026 · OpenAI's revised usage policy took effect November 20: routers and gateways reselling model access need prior approval, outputs may not build training data or graders for substitute models, 'protected reasoning' may not be extracted; GPT-6 Rosalind listed at $10 / $50 through December 31

The changes were posted to OpenAI's changelog on October 21 with 30 days' notice and replace the October 2025 policy. Platforms that give third parties direct access to OpenAI models must now be reviewed and approved, first-party apps are exempt, and the ban on training competitors widens to rubrics, labels, reinforcement-learning environments and data filtering. Separately, OpenAI's pricing page now shows GPT-6 Rosalind at $10 per million input and $50 output, a promotional rate through at least December 31, down from the $15 / $75 we tracked at launch; it also confirms cached input at $0.40 for GPT-6.2 Astra and $0.10 for GPT-6.2 Sol.

FinanceRosalind's cut is a third off the biomedical model's list price until year end; the tracker row is updated. Cached-input rates on the 6.2 models are 5% of input price.

The benchmark, published Friday, draws 99 problems from 117 papers and patents covering 761 molecules and grades structure reconstruction, evidence-based reasoning, compound selection and numerical prediction in a single tool-free attempt; OpenAI says the scores do not show a model can find a drug. Narasimhan, who also sits on Anthropic's board, told an interviewer over the weekend that biology is understood far less well than code, and that the realistic prize is shortening a twelve-year development cycle toward eight. Ant Group's health unit released a 560-billion-parameter open-weight medical reasoning model on Sunday.

FinanceIsomorphic's $5 billion round and Narasimhan's scepticism price the same uncertainty differently. The benchmark data supports caution for now.
