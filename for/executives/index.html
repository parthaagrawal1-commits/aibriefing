Nov 9, 2026 · Claude Code's 'don't ask again' button put 2,539 secrets in public GitHub repos

Security firm Apiiro found that when a Claude Code user approves a shell command with 'Yes, and don't ask again', the exact command is saved to .claude/settings.local.json in the project, including any password or token typed into it. Scanning 104,185 public copies of the file, Apiiro found 1,122 containing 1,379 live-looking secrets, including 379 database passwords and 23 AWS keys; with older versions and transcripts the total reached 2,539 secrets across 1,994 repositories. Claude Code head Boris Cherny said on Hacker News that Anthropic is looking into it and that the tool has not historically scrubbed secrets.

ExecutivesThis is what 'shadow AI' looks like in practice: a convenience feature, not a breach, and the fix is a policy plus a scanner rule. Ask whether your coding-agent rollout included either.
Source: The Hacker News · iTnews

Nov 9, 2026 · Check Point: autonomous ARTEX agents hit 200+ organisations in 14 countries since October 17

An AI-driven intrusion campaign using the ARTEX penetration-testing framework has targeted more than 200 organisations across 14 countries since October 17, compromising at least nine, BleepingComputer reported from CrowdStrike and Check Point research. The agents forged an administrator token to read a Greek ministry's mailboxes, skipped hosts protected by CrowdStrike Falcon on operator instruction, and burned more than 1.4 billion input tokens in a single intrusion. Separately, the Dutch vulnerability-disclosure institute DIVD said ARTEX agents went from initial access to root on its systems in 12 minutes.

ExecutivesNation-state capability is now available as an open-source framework plus a commercial API key. Your exposure is defined by unpatched internet-facing systems, not by whether you are a 'target'.
Source: BleepingComputer · Help Net Security (OpenSSH exploitation)

Nov 9, 2026 · Thinking Machines ships Inkling 2 Small: 594B-parameter MoE, 17B active, weights promised under Apache 2.0

Thinking Machines Lab released Inkling 2 Small on November 5, a sparse mixture-of-experts model with 594 billion total and 17 billion active parameters, a 1-million-token context window, and image input. It was pretrained on 8.4 trillion tokens in 68 hours on Nvidia GB300 racks using native NVFP4, then trained with reinforcement learning for nine days on 2,048 GPUs. Inference is available through the company's Tinker platform at a listed $0.30 per million input tokens and $1.00 output; full weights are promised under Apache 2.0 within weeks, conditional on external safety testing.

ExecutivesA second-generation open-weight model from a two-year-old lab, with a staged release gated on red-teaming, is the new normal. Open weights are not a side show to the closed labs any more.
Source: Thinking Machines Lab · Unite.AI

Nov 9, 2026 · Cassidy's PATIENT AI Act draft would add a third-party FDA review path for health AI; comments due December 1

Senate HELP Committee chair Bill Cassidy's discussion draft, released October 29 with stakeholder comments requested by December 1, proposes a voluntary FDA preliminary review for software components of 'integrated devices', an accredited third-party review programme for low- and moderate-risk software devices, pre-submitted change-control plans, and a Medicare coverage pathway for AI services. An analysis by the FDA Law Blog published November 8 argues much of the FDA title duplicates authority the agency already has and could add procedure without shortening time to market.

ExecutivesIf you sell or buy clinical AI, this is the first serious attempt to legislate how it gets reviewed and paid for. Three weeks remain to shape it.
