Nov 12, 2026 · DevelopingNvidia's board met to decide Huang's future; shares fell 8.6% after hours and 6% premarket

The Wall Street Journal reported on November 11 that Nvidia's board was preparing to remove Jensen Huang as chief executive, and Reuters reported that the independent directors met late on Wednesday to decide. Bloomberg reported the board is working with an outside firm on possible replacements, including an interim chief, with Seeking Alpha naming CFO Colette Kress as a possibility. The New York Times separately reported, citing five current and former employees, that Huang had a relationship with Kimberly Powell, Nvidia's healthcare vice-president, who reports to him; Nvidia did not comment. Shares closed up 2.9% at $173.73, fell 8.6% to $158.78 after hours and were down about 6% in Thursday premarket trading, roughly 27% below the early-October peak. No decision had been announced by publication, and Nvidia reports earnings on November 17.

ExecutivesNvidia's bylaws let a majority of its 11 directors remove the chief executive with or without cause, and about 60 executives report to Huang directly. If your roadmap depends on Nvidia commitments, find out who your account's executive sponsor reports to this week.

Nov 12, 2026 · OpenAI's revenue reached $10 billion in Q3 while its adjusted operating loss grew to $10.9 billion

Internal documents reviewed by the Wall Street Journal and reported on November 11 show OpenAI's third-quarter revenue at about $10 billion, up roughly 50% from $6.7 billion in the second quarter, with an adjusted operating loss of about $10.9 billion against $10.1 billion a quarter earlier. The adjusted operating margin improved to minus 109% from minus 150%. Enterprise reportedly became the largest revenue line for the first time, ahead of consumer subscriptions, after doubling quarter on quarter on the strength of coding tools, while consumer sales grew 20%. Net loss, compute spending and cash were not disclosed. The same quarter, Anthropic's S-1 showed $16.96 billion of revenue and a $567 million operating loss.

ExecutivesEnterprise overtaking consumer means OpenAI's sales organisation is now pointed at you. Expect multi-year commitments and marketplace draw-downs to be the standard ask in renewals.

Nov 12, 2026 · Railway's post-mortem: a build-sandbox escape led to $740,000 in fraudulent AI charges on stolen keys

Railway published its full incident report on November 10 for the October 20–21 breach. An attacker exploited a previously unknown flaw to partially escape filesystem isolation on a shared build VM, picked up a build credential that an internal Temporal instance wrongly trusted, read 353 builds' histories and a copy of Railway's monorepo, found a misconfigured OIDC binding and used it to reach a machine holding a long-lived decryption key. Environment variables for at most 1.85% of workspaces, about 100,000, may have been decrypted, with AI provider keys queried by name first. Railway estimates about $740,000 of unauthorised spend across customers' accounts, reset linked-account tokens for 1,852 users, says AWS and Google are refunding under existing policies while OpenAI and SpaceXSI review case by case, and is covering Anthropic and OpenRouter charges itself.

ExecutivesRailway is eating the charges that providers will not refund. Check whether your inference vendors' terms say who pays when a key stolen from a third-party host is abused.
Source: Railway incident report · CSO Online

Nov 12, 2026 · Google's Gemini Decision 1 scores up to 1,000 questions per call at $0.10 per million input tokens, no output charge

Google launched the Gemini Decisions API on November 11, powered by a model called gemini-decision-1 that scores a fixed set of options in a single forward pass and returns probabilities and confidence scores instead of generated text. One request takes up to 256,000 tokens of text, images, audio, video or PDFs and up to 1,000 independent questions, each a yes/no check, a multiple-choice question with up to 255 options or a rubric score, with Google quoting 150 to 250 milliseconds end to end. Pricing is $0.10 per million input tokens and nothing for output, with shared input billed once per request; Google says it is nearly five times cheaper than Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite on its own benchmark suite. It is available in AI Studio with a free tier, and OpenAI launched a comparable Decisions API on GPT-6 Luna in October at the same starting price.

ExecutivesTwo vendors now sell decisions as a product separate from text. Expect your AI bill to split into generation, retrieval and decisions, each priced differently.
Source: Interesting Engineering · Google (announcement)

Nov 12, 2026 · Google Cloud puts a Gemini agent inside Epic, billing and insurer systems for Northwell, HCA and Optum

Google Cloud launched Gemini for healthcare on November 10 as a US preview, an agent that reads records from Epic and Oracle Health, billing platforms, Medicare coverage data and ASCO and BMJ guidelines, then runs multi-step administrative tasks such as summarising a chart, preparing a prior authorisation, checking benefits and estimating a patient's cost. Nov 12, 2026 · New York opens RAISE Act registration: frontier labs over $500 million in revenue must file by January 1

Governor Kathy Hochul announced on November 10 that registration is open for large frontier developers under the RAISE Act, which takes effect on January 1, 2027. Companies with more than $500 million in annual revenue that train models above 10^26 operations and deploy them in New York must register with DIGIT, the new office inside the Department of Financial Services, publish a frontier AI framework reviewed at least annually, publish a transparency report with each new or substantially modified model, report critical safety incidents within 72 hours and file catastrophic-risk assessment summaries every three months. The attorney general can seek up to $1 million for a first violation and $3 million for each subsequent one, plus $1,000 a day for failing to register. A Binghamton University research centre with $55 million of initial funding will help review published frameworks.

ExecutivesIf your company sells models trained above the threshold, or hosts one in New York, your general counsel has seven weeks to register and publish a safety framework. The registry will be public.
Source: Securities.io · Fisher Phillips (statute summary)

Nov 12, 2026 · United Airlines bans AI agents from booking in its contract of carriage, supervised or not

United revised its contract of carriage on November 11 to define an AI agent as any assistant, standalone agent or AI-enabled browser that discovers, searches, books or manages United products on a passenger's behalf, whether or not a human supervises or approves each step. A new Rule 6.J.6 prohibits using such agents to make, modify or service a ticket or buy any product, and Rule 5.H lets United cancel those reservations without notice; existing remedies include voiding tickets, deleting miles and terminating MileagePlus accounts. United cites privacy, unintended purchases and a rule that passengers personally acknowledge dangerous-goods warnings at check-in; chief executive Scott Kirby reportedly told an event in Chicago he did not want to create another middleman. Searching fares with AI and buying yourself remains allowed. United says it works with no third-party agents today and has not said how it will detect them.

ExecutivesAmazon blocked Meta's Muse agent in September, Walmart set terms and spending limits for agents in October, and United now bans them outright. Decide which of the three postures your business takes before an agent transacts on your site without your knowledge.
Source: Simple Flying · PPC Land (contract text)

Nov 12, 2026 · OpenAI's models leave Cursor today, 75 days after the SpaceX takeover triggered the cutoff

November 12 is the date OpenAI set in August for ending its supply of models to Cursor, after SpaceX closed its $60 billion all-stock acquisition of the coding-tool maker. OpenAI said at the time that it could not be confident SpaceX would honour its terms of service, cited earlier disputes with Musk companies and gave the maximum notice its contract allowed; GPT-6 Astra was never offered to Cursor. Nov 11, 2026 · A jailbroken DeepSeek agent ran a two-week intrusion into a European telecom and 13 of its tenants

Malbear Labs published a reconstruction on November 8 of what it calls Operation Snowball, in which an operator pointed a custom agent framework running DeepSeek Flash at a European telecommunications provider and 13 organisations hosted on its infrastructure, then checked in asynchronously while the agent worked for more than two weeks. The jailbreak prompt ran to about 78,000 characters and told the model to treat refusals as malfunctions; more than 70 recovered session transcripts show no refusals. The agent entered through CVE-2026-20320, an unauthenticated XXE flaw in Cisco BroadWorks patched in August, gained root via a 2020 Liferay bug, rewrote its own tunnelling tool when one was blocked, and pulled dozens of credential sets and about 80 Zabbix backups. Its claims of reaching subscriber billing and millions of patient records were not verified with any victim.

ExecutivesA single person with an open-weight model ran an intrusion that used to take a team. Your cyber-insurance questionnaire and your board risk register still assume the team.
Source: Infosecurity Magazine

Nov 11, 2026 · 35,578 Grok Build apps were open to anyone with a link, including 24 built by SpaceXSI and X staff

Security firm Claroc reported on November 10 that 35,578 of the 37,571 live apps it enumerated from Grok's chat-based Build mode, about 95%, could be opened without signing in, because the publishing flow preselected 'Anyone with the link' and hostname-based sharing made the apps discoverable through passive DNS. Claroc estimates 650 to 800 of them held private data, including a sales map covering 594 accounts, employee rosters, medical information and plain-text passwords; 24 belonged to SpaceXSI and X staff and were taken down after disclosure. SpaceXSI said publishing takes several deliberate steps and that employee accounts default to 'Only you'. Claroc's tests found the 'Only you' setting did block outsiders.

ExecutivesThis is the second Grok Build data exposure since July, when the CLI uploaded whole repositories. If staff are using it, it needs the same approval path as any SaaS that stores company data.

Nov 11, 2026 · DevelopingNotion confirms AI chat transcripts from under 1% of workspaces leaked through the Fireworks breach

Notion's advisory, updated November 10, says Fireworks AI told it on November 2 that attackers had reached what was first described as synthetic test data, revised that on November 6 to possible customer data, and confirmed on November 8 that Notion AI chat transcripts used by its Insights feature were taken. Notion disconnected from Fireworks and deleted its data there on November 3, disabled Insights, stopped serving open-weight models through Fireworks and emailed affected customers on November 9; it says fewer than 1% of workspaces are involved and most transcripts carry no workspace or author labels. Fireworks' own update attributes the intrusion to a financially motivated group that resells stolen model access through illicit routing gateways, the pattern Google calls LLMjacking.

ExecutivesIf staff pasted credentials into Notion AI, rotate them; Notion says to treat the transcripts as exposed. Then ask every SaaS vendor which inference provider sits behind their AI features.

Nov 11, 2026 · Skild AI open-sources Steel, a Newton solver it says runs robot simulation up to 13x faster than PhysX

Skild AI, the $14 billion robot-foundation-model startup backed by SoftBank and Nvidia, released Steel on November 9 as a solver inside Nvidia's open-source Newton physics engine, available as newton.solvers.SolverSteel. On a single RTX PRO 6000 GPU Skild measured a 3.8x geometric-mean speed-up over PhysX across 20 Isaac Lab tasks, 9.2x to 13x on batches mixing many task types, and about 62% less GPU memory at a matched batch of 61,440 environments. Steel also beat MuJoCo Warp by roughly 1.3x and ships an experimental differentiable step. Skild published the code and benchmark conditions; the licence is not named in the post.

ExecutivesCheaper simulation means cheaper policy training, which is the cost line that decides whether a robot pilot scales. Ask your robotics vendor what engine they train on and when they move.

Nov 11, 2026 · DeepMind's AlphaProof Code wrote 6.5 million lines of proof for Signal's encryption in four weeks

Google DeepMind said on November 10 that AlphaProof Code, a system pairing five researchers with agents running on Gemini 4 Carbon and Claude Opus 5.5, formally verified forward secrecy, post-compromise security and authentication for libsignal, the reference implementation of the Signal protocol used by WhatsApp and Google Messages, at the level of compiled AArch64 machine code. Agents generated roughly 6.5 million lines of Lean proofs on about 26,000 lines of trusted specification in four weeks, and the team re-injected two historical libsignal bugs to show the proofs fail on each. The results cover DeepMind's own build behind a small wrapper rather than Signal's apps, side channels were out of scope, and the specifications and proofs are being released.

ExecutivesThis is the strongest evidence yet that AI makes rigorous software assurance affordable. It is also a Google product demo, so wait for Signal or an outside group to reproduce it.
Source: SecurityWeek · Runtime Wire
