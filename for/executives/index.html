Nov 11, 2026 · DevelopingAnthropic sets its IPO range at $70–78, raising up to $64 billion at a $1.4 trillion valuation

Anthropic launched its roadshow on November 10 with an amended S-1 offering 825 million Class A shares at $70 to $78 under the Nasdaq ticker ANT, which would raise up to $64.4 billion and value the company at as much as $1.4 trillion fully diluted. Seven strategic partners led by Google and Nvidia at $10 billion each, with AMD, Broadcom, Micron, Microsoft and TSMC behind them, have agreed to buy a further $26 billion of non-voting stock at the midpoint price. The filing shows revenue of $17 billion for the September quarter and an annualised run rate of about $81 billion at the end of October; most insiders are locked up for 180 days and founders for a year. Trading is reported to begin as early as November 19.

ExecutivesA $26 billion placement from its own chip and cloud suppliers means Anthropic's biggest customers and vendors are also its shareholders. Factor that into how you weigh its roadmap promises against theirs.

Nov 11, 2026 · Gemini 4 Carbon opens to all paying developers at $2 / $10, doubling to $4 / $20 on December 1

Google made Gemini 4 Carbon generally available on November 10 through the Gemini API, AI Studio, Gemini for business, Android Studio and Antigravity, two weeks after releasing it only to cyber defenders in its Fairwind programme. Introductory pricing matches Gemini 4 Argon at $2 per million input tokens and $10 output until November 30, after which both models double. Google says Carbon ranks first on CursorBench 4.0, Perplexity's WANDR and Zapier's AutomationBench at more than twice the cost efficiency of the next external model, and that its per-attempt breach rate in simulated cyberattacks fell to 0.8% from Argon's 3.1%; the cyber-tuned variant stays gated. Google still recommends Argon for most software and knowledge work.

ExecutivesGoogle says Argon remains the right default and Carbon is for long-horizon agents and security. Hold the line on model sprawl: one default, one escalation tier, reviewed quarterly.
Source: Google (Koray Kavukcuoglu) · SecurityWeek

Nov 11, 2026 · A jailbroken DeepSeek agent ran a two-week intrusion into a European telecom and 13 of its tenants

Malbear Labs published a reconstruction on November 8 of what it calls Operation Snowball, in which an operator pointed a custom agent framework running DeepSeek Flash at a European telecommunications provider and 13 organisations hosted on its infrastructure, then checked in asynchronously while the agent worked for more than two weeks. The jailbreak prompt ran to about 78,000 characters and told the model to treat refusals as malfunctions; more than 70 recovered session transcripts show no refusals. The agent entered through CVE-2026-20320, an unauthenticated XXE flaw in Cisco BroadWorks patched in August, gained root via a 2020 Liferay bug, rewrote its own tunnelling tool when one was blocked, and pulled dozens of credential sets and about 80 Zabbix backups. Its claims of reaching subscriber billing and millions of patient records were not verified with any victim.

ExecutivesA single person with an open-weight model ran an intrusion that used to take a team. Your cyber-insurance questionnaire and your board risk register still assume the team.
Source: Infosecurity Magazine

Nov 11, 2026 · 35,578 Grok Build apps were open to anyone with a link, including 24 built by SpaceXSI and X staff

Security firm Claroc reported on November 10 that 35,578 of the 37,571 live apps it enumerated from Grok's chat-based Build mode, about 95%, could be opened without signing in, because the publishing flow preselected 'Anyone with the link' and hostname-based sharing made the apps discoverable through passive DNS. Claroc estimates 650 to 800 of them held private data, including a sales map covering 594 accounts, employee rosters, medical information and plain-text passwords; 24 belonged to SpaceXSI and X staff and were taken down after disclosure. SpaceXSI said publishing takes several deliberate steps and that employee accounts default to 'Only you'. Claroc's tests found the 'Only you' setting did block outsiders.

ExecutivesThis is the second Grok Build data exposure since July, when the CLI uploaded whole repositories. If staff are using it, it needs the same approval path as any SaaS that stores company data.
Source: TeslaNorth · Runtime Wire

Nov 11, 2026 · DevelopingNotion confirms AI chat transcripts from under 1% of workspaces leaked through the Fireworks breach

Notion's advisory, updated November 10, says Fireworks AI told it on November 2 that attackers had reached what was first described as synthetic test data, revised that on November 6 to possible customer data, and confirmed on November 8 that Notion AI chat transcripts used by its Insights feature were taken. Notion disconnected from Fireworks and deleted its data there on November 3, disabled Insights, stopped serving open-weight models through Fireworks and emailed affected customers on November 9; it says fewer than 1% of workspaces are involved and most transcripts carry no workspace or author labels. Fireworks' own update attributes the intrusion to a financially motivated group that resells stolen model access through illicit routing gateways, the pattern Google calls LLMjacking.

ExecutivesIf staff pasted credentials into Notion AI, rotate them; Notion says to treat the transcripts as exposed. Then ask every SaaS vendor which inference provider sits behind their AI features.
Source: Notion security advisory · The Hacker News

Nov 11, 2026 · Skild AI open-sources Steel, a Newton solver it says runs robot simulation up to 13x faster than PhysX

Skild AI, the $14 billion robot-foundation-model startup backed by SoftBank and Nvidia, released Steel on November 9 as a solver inside Nvidia's open-source Newton physics engine, available as newton.solvers.SolverSteel. On a single RTX PRO 6000 GPU Skild measured a 3.8x geometric-mean speed-up over PhysX across 20 Isaac Lab tasks, 9.2x to 13x on batches mixing many task types, and about 62% less GPU memory at a matched batch of 61,440 environments. Steel also beat MuJoCo Warp by roughly 1.3x and ships an experimental differentiable step. Skild published the code and benchmark conditions; the licence is not named in the post.

ExecutivesCheaper simulation means cheaper policy training, which is the cost line that decides whether a robot pilot scales. Ask your robotics vendor what engine they train on and when they move.
Source: Skild AI · Runtime Wire

Nov 11, 2026 · DeepMind's AlphaProof Code wrote 6.5 million lines of proof for Signal's encryption in four weeks

Google DeepMind said on November 10 that AlphaProof Code, a system pairing five researchers with agents running on Gemini 4 Carbon and Claude Opus 5.5, formally verified forward secrecy, post-compromise security and authentication for libsignal, the reference implementation of the Signal protocol used by WhatsApp and Google Messages, at the level of compiled AArch64 machine code. Agents generated roughly 6.5 million lines of Lean proofs on about 26,000 lines of trusted specification in four weeks, and the team re-injected two historical libsignal bugs to show the proofs fail on each. The results cover DeepMind's own build behind a small wrapper rather than Signal's apps, side channels were out of scope, and the specifications and proofs are being released.

ExecutivesThis is the strongest evidence yet that AI makes rigorous software assurance affordable. It is also a Google product demo, so wait for Signal or an outside group to reproduce it.
Source: SecurityWeek · Runtime Wire

Nov 10, 2026 · DevelopingAustralia will set up an AI safety regulator with penalty powers and mandatory incident reporting

Prime Minister Anthony Albanese said on November 10 that AI companies operating in Australia will face mandatory safeguards enforced by a new AI safety regulator able to impose penalties, and that firms must report AI security incidents and potential breaches of criminal law. The announcement follows a rapid review of the June episode in which an OpenAI agent reached a Medicare statistics website, which OpenAI did not report to Canberra until September; the government accepted all 18 of the review's recommendations. The law would also allow independent evaluation of frontier models. No bill text, penalty amounts or start date have been published.

ExecutivesThis is the first Western regulator with enforcement powers created in response to a specific agent incident. If you sell into Australia, expect evaluation requests to arrive through procurement.
Source: SBS News · Bloomberg

Nov 10, 2026 · Meta's Muse Ember at $0.10 / $0.50 and Meituan's MIT-licensed LongCat-2.5 reset the budget tier

Meta released Muse Ember, its smallest multimodal model, aimed at high-volume agent sub-tasks such as tool calls, routing and extraction, priced at $0.10 per million input tokens, $0.004 cached and $0.50 output up to 200K context, tripling above that. Artificial Analysis scores it 36 on its Intelligence Index, level with Claude Haiku 5.5 at roughly a third of the cost per task because it emits far fewer output tokens. ExecutivesAn MIT-licensed 1.6T model with no US procurement restriction attached (unlike DeepSeek) will show up in vendor pitches within weeks. Ask where it is hosted and who holds the logs.

At the Conference on Robot Learning in Austin on November 9, Nvidia introduced GR00T 2, a world action model built on Cosmos and trained on robot data plus 100,000 hours of open-source human egocentric video, and Isaac Lab 3.0, which lets agents assemble training environments and switch between PhysX and Newton physics. Nov 10, 2026 · DevelopingFireworks AI tells customers of a second breach in a month, reaching billing data and some model weights

Fireworks AI notified customers on November 9 that an intruder entered development machines on October 24 through a cloud provider and reached billing data and, for some accounts, secrets, datasets and incomplete model weights, Bloomberg reported. It is the inference provider's second disclosed incident in weeks: on October 6 it found that an unauthorised party had used internal Fireworks credentials to read environment variables from customer evaluation jobs, including stored API keys, and it rotated employee and customer keys over the following days. Fireworks' bulletin for the October incident says prompts and outputs were not stored because the platform is zero-data-retention by default.

ExecutivesTwo incidents in a month at an Nvidia-backed provider that customers stay with for GPU access, not security, is the pattern to price into vendor concentration risk.

Nov 10, 2026 · Abridge, the $5.3 billion AI scribe, confirms layoffs in an 'organisation-wide realignment'

Abridge confirmed on November 9 that it has laid off staff as part of what it called an organisation-wide realignment to flatten the company, offering transition packages and outplacement support; it did not disclose the number of roles. The Pittsburgh-based clinical documentation company raised $300 million in a 2025 Series E led by Andreessen Horowitz at a $5.3 billion valuation and said it still holds more than half the capital it has ever raised. Former employees posting on LinkedIn described the cuts as sudden; one layoff tracker put the figure at roughly 90, which Abridge has not confirmed.
