Nov 10, 2026 · Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2 arrive at $8 / $40, 20% below the 5.1 price

Anthropic released Claude Fable 5.2 and Claude Mythos 5.2 on November 9 at $8 per million input tokens and $40 output, down from $10 / $50 for the 5.1 generation, with cached input at $0.20 and a new fast mode at $16 / $80. Anthropic's own tables put Fable 5.2 at 49.1% on FrontierCode 2.0 against 32.4% for 5.1, and the company says output is up to 30% faster. Fable is generally available on the Claude API, AWS, Google Cloud and Azure; Mythos remains limited to vetted organisations in cyber and life sciences, and Anthropic still recommends Opus 5.5 as the default for most work.

DevelopersAnthropic says Fable 5.2 at High effort roughly matches Opus 5.5 on FrontierCode at about half the cost per task. Re-run your own comparison this week rather than trusting either number.

Nov 10, 2026 · Meta's Muse Ember at $0.10 / $0.50 and Meituan's MIT-licensed LongCat-2.5 reset the budget tier

Meta released Muse Ember, its smallest multimodal model, aimed at high-volume agent sub-tasks such as tool calls, routing and extraction, priced at $0.10 per million input tokens, $0.004 cached and $0.50 output up to 200K context, tripling above that. Artificial Analysis scores it 36 on its Intelligence Index, level with Claude Haiku 5.5 at roughly a third of the cost per task because it emits far fewer output tokens. Separately, Meituan published the weights of LongCat-2.5, a 1.6-trillion-parameter mixture-of-experts model with about 48 billion active parameters, under the MIT licence on November 9, with hosted pricing of $0.30 / $1.20.

DevelopersEmber's cached-input price of $0.004 is 60% below Haiku's; for a retrieval or routing loop that reuses a long prefix, that is where the saving is. Mind the 3x surcharge above 200K tokens.
Source: Meta developer page · Artificial Analysis · VentureBeat

Nov 10, 2026 · DevelopingFireworks AI tells customers of a second breach in a month, reaching billing data and some model weights

Fireworks AI notified customers on November 9 that an intruder entered development machines on October 24 through a cloud provider and reached billing data and, for some accounts, secrets, datasets and incomplete model weights, Bloomberg reported. It is the inference provider's second disclosed incident in weeks: on October 6 it found that an unauthorised party had used internal Fireworks credentials to read environment variables from customer evaluation jobs, including stored API keys, and it rotated employee and customer keys over the following days. Fireworks' bulletin for the October incident says prompts and outputs were not stored because the platform is zero-data-retention by default.

DevelopersDeleting a stored secret does not revoke it at the issuer. Script rotation for the keys your eval and fine-tuning jobs use and keep it ready for the next vendor notice.
Source: Runtime Wire (on Bloomberg) · Fireworks AI security bulletin

Nov 9, 2026 · Claude Code's 'don't ask again' button put 2,539 secrets in public GitHub repos

Security firm Apiiro found that when a Claude Code user approves a shell command with 'Yes, and don't ask again', the exact command is saved to .claude/settings.local.json in the project, including any password or token typed into it. Scanning 104,185 public copies of the file, Apiiro found 1,122 containing 1,379 live-looking secrets, including 379 database passwords and 23 AWS keys; with older versions and transcripts the total reached 2,539 secrets across 1,994 repositories. Claude Code head Boris Cherny said on Hacker News that Anthropic is looking into it and that the tool has not historically scrubbed secrets.

DevelopersSearch your org for .claude/settings.local.json in git history, rotate anything it ever contained, then untrack it and approve command patterns (Bash(npm run:*)) instead of literal commands with credentials in them.
Source: The Hacker News · iTnews

Nov 9, 2026 · Check Point: autonomous ARTEX agents hit 200+ organisations in 14 countries since October 17

An AI-driven intrusion campaign using the ARTEX penetration-testing framework has targeted more than 200 organisations across 14 countries since October 17, compromising at least nine, BleepingComputer reported from CrowdStrike and Check Point research. The agents forged an administrator token to read a Greek ministry's mailboxes, skipped hosts protected by CrowdStrike Falcon on operator instruction, and burned more than 1.4 billion input tokens in a single intrusion. Separately, the Dutch vulnerability-disclosure institute DIVD said ARTEX agents went from initial access to root on its systems in 12 minutes.

DevelopersThe 1.4 billion tokens in one intrusion were paid for by someone. Monitor your API spend and key usage for runaway agent loops; a stolen key is now a weapon as well as a cost.
Source: BleepingComputer · Help Net Security (OpenSSH exploitation)

Nov 9, 2026 · Thinking Machines ships Inkling 2 Small: 594B-parameter MoE, 17B active, weights promised under Apache 2.0

Thinking Machines Lab released Inkling 2 Small on November 5, a sparse mixture-of-experts model with 594 billion total and 17 billion active parameters, a 1-million-token context window, and image input. It was pretrained on 8.4 trillion tokens in 68 hours on Nvidia GB300 racks using native NVFP4, then trained with reinforcement learning for nine days on 2,048 GPUs. Inference is available through the company's Tinker platform at a listed $0.30 per million input tokens and $1.00 output; full weights are promised under Apache 2.0 within weeks, conditional on external safety testing.

DevelopersAt $0.30 / $1.00 this undercuts every current closed mid-tier by a wide margin, but it is only on Tinker for now. Worth a benchmark on your workload, not a migration.
Source: Thinking Machines Lab · Unite.AI

OpenAI's pricing page confirms that promotional pricing on GPT-5.6 Sol ($4 input / $20 output per million tokens) runs only until November 30, after which the model returns to its $5 / $30 list rate. Nov 8, 2026 · OpenAI launches GPT-6 Cyber and rates it 'Critical' for cybersecurity capability
