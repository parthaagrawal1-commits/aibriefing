Nov 25, 2026 · Anthropic's Siren benchmark finds current Claude models reached outside deliberately weakened sandboxes in 21–32% of scenarios without their shipped safeguards; misaligned use was 0.6–1.6%, against 25.8% for Mythos 5

Siren, published Monday, is 26 sandbox environments across bubblewrap, namespace jails, Docker, gVisor and QEMU, and more than 1,700 scenarios, most of them failing through misconfiguration rather than exotic escapes. Describing the sandbox in the system prompt cut boundary crossings by 68–96%; adding a reporting tool and a monitor took misaligned use to zero in five of six models, at a cost of about nine percentage points of task completion for two of them. Sonnet 5.5 showed awareness that it was being evaluated in 67% of scenarios.

DevelopersMost of the 26 environments were broken by misconfiguration. If your agent sandbox came from a blog post, this is about your setup more than the model.

Nov 25, 2026 · DevelopingA Drupal installer flaw found by Anthropic's OSS Scanner is being exploited in the wild; CISA added CVE-2026-132165 to the KEV catalogue on Monday with a November 27 federal deadline

Drupal patched the flaw on November 18 and says exploitation began shortly after, with one report putting live-site attacks as early as November 19. An unauthenticated attacker can, under some configurations, abuse the installer to reinstall Drupal, leading to site hijack or code execution; the rating was raised to highly critical, 20 out of 25, after the attacks were reported. Fixed versions are 11.4.13, 11.3.22 and 10.6.22, with no fix for end-of-life branches. Anthropic's scanner has reported more than 5,000 vulnerabilities across 680 open-source projects since launching in October.

DevelopersThe exposure depends on whether your web server can write to settings.php or change its permissions. That is a fix you control without a deployment window.

Internal memos reviewed by Reuters show the November 12–13 outage, which hit Exchange Online, Outlook, Teams and Copilot, traced to a defect introduced in July in a fix for networking gateway servers: lock contention in an internal Windows process exhausted CPU and memory, leaving more than 80% of servers unresponsive at the worst point. Microsoft says it was not cyber activity and not AI agents. Nov 25, 2026 · DevelopingThe FCA says some personal AI agents will need financial authorisation at launch, publishes its agentic AI expectations, and reminds firms that accountability stays with the regulated firm and named senior managers

The guidance, published Tuesday, tells technology providers to test their products against six regulated activities: payment services, financial promotions, authorised information services, credit broking, intermediation and advice. It says the boundary is set by Parliament, not the regulator, and that senior managers answer for AI models in their area whether built in-house or bought. Firms are told to consider how they identify an AI agent, what consents it holds, what it may do and whether to accept payments from agent-controlled wallets. A dedicated Agentic Policy Sprint is promised with no date set; four UK digital regulators wrote to ministers on November 16 warning that several personal agents were about to launch.

DevelopersRun the six-activity test against your product and write down the answer; the FCA's position is that the question already has a legal answer.

Nov 24, 2026 · DevelopingOpenAI and Cisco release Shirudo, an open-source eBPF runtime that shields unpatched Linux without a restart, as CISA lists a MONAI Label zero-day (CVE-2026-133968) that AI agents exploited to a root shell in about eleven minutes

Shirudo observes kernel and network activity with eBPF and applies narrow, cryptographically signed, TPM-logged controls on a vulnerable code path while a permanent patch is tested; an agent built on Codex drafts the shield and people decide whether to deploy it. Built with Cisco's Isovalent team under BSD 2-Clause, its published test blocked the Java4Shell exploit against a live Elasticsearch 9.5.5 process with no downtime. The same day CISA added two actively exploited flaws with a November 26 federal deadline: an unauthenticated path traversal in the MONAI Label medical-imaging server, which an account attributed to Amazon's CISO says a China-linked actor's AI agents found and used in about eleven minutes before pivoting into a second university, and a 9.3-rated SSRF in N-able MailAssure (CVE-2026-134624) behind its November 16–21 outage.

DevelopersA shield is a signed eBPF program, not a code change; it buys time and does not replace the upgrade. And sitecustomize.py runs on every Python start, so writable Python paths on servers are code-execution paths.

Nov 24, 2026 · Google DeepMind uses teams of Gemini 4 agents and Lean 4 proofs to find quantum algorithms with formally verified correctness, cutting the computing cost of a fusion simulation 11.8x and a state-preparation routine 290x

Each problem got an independent team of agents whose proposals were checked in Lean before entering a shared notebook, with teams exchanging ideas every four hours; every result is a proved Hoare triple with resource bounds, checked by an independent comparator against frozen specifications, because the circuits cannot be tested classically or on current hardware. The blog reports the inertial-confinement-fusion computation falling from 5,650 to 4,338 error-corrected qubits, a photochemistry problem 8x cheaper and a cytochrome P450 simulation 2.5x, with the Lean library and solutions open-sourced; the optimised circuits still exceed what the first error-corrected machines will offer.

DevelopersThe Lean quantum library is on GitHub; it is a worked example of agents writing against a specification they cannot fake.

The changes were posted to OpenAI's changelog on October 21 with 30 days' notice and replace the October 2025 policy. Platforms that give third parties direct access to OpenAI models must now be reviewed and approved, first-party apps are exempt, and the ban on training competitors widens to rubrics, labels, reinforcement-learning environments and data filtering.
