Nov 14, 2026 · OpenAI launches ChatGPT Wallet: dots agents pay with Visa, Mastercard and Amex network tokens, one biometric approval per purchase, no OpenAI cut at launch

ChatGPT Wallet, live on Thursday for a limited set of US ChatGPT Pro subscribers aged 18 and over, lets a dot find items on a merchant's site, present a review screen of items, total and card, and complete checkout after the user approves with Face ID or a fingerprint. The card network issues a limited-use token with its own number, security code and expiry, so neither the agent nor the merchant sees the real card; rewards and dispute protections carry over. There are no spending limits or pre-approved purchases yet, OpenAI says it takes no share of transactions for now, and purchases fail on sites that block agents. OpenAI published a Commerce Partner Playbook and named Adyen, Checkout.com, Stripe and fraud vendors Forter, Riskified, Sardine and Signifyd as partners.

DevelopersMerchant work is concrete: let verified agent visits through, publish product feeds, read the Forwarded header for locale and device, add Sign in with ChatGPT. The playbook is the spec.

Nov 14, 2026 · IBM ships Granite 4.1 'LLMON' previews that bind instructions to data with reserved tokens, claiming near-zero indirect prompt-injection success on OPI

IBM published experimental 3B and 8B Granite 4.1 models trained on LLM Object Notation, an XML-like markup whose delimiters are reserved special tokens and whose tags mark instructions, data and requirements, with unique IDs tying each instruction to the data it may act on. On the OPI indirect-injection benchmark IBM reports attack success near zero, avoiding 47% more attacks than the best frontier model it tested and 95% more than the best open model, with similar trends on ASB and StruQ and no loss on standard utility benchmarks. IBM calls it a preview not for production, says the resistance is learned rather than enforced, and has not yet tested adaptive multi-turn attacks.

DevelopersJSON converts to LLMON and back losslessly, so wrapping an existing tool-calling pipeline is a formatting change, not a rewrite. The toolkit and templates are on GitHub.
Source: IBM Granite on Hugging Face

Nov 14, 2026 · llama.cpp and ggml reach v1.0.0 after 3.5 years: semantic versioning, 35 deprecated functions removed, 150+ model architectures

Georgi Gerganov's ggml-org released llama.cpp 1.0.0 and ggml 1.0.0 on Thursday with a formal promise: breaking changes to the public C API or ABI only arrive in major versions, minor versions add backends and models, patches fix bugs. The release removes the deprecated API including 35 functions, promotes the speculative-decoding API to the public header, adds FP8 KV caches and a host-memory cache for mixture-of-experts models, and ships packages for Linux, macOS, Windows, Android and iOS via Docker, Debian, Homebrew, conda-forge, Winget and an XCFramework. Nvidia, AMD, Qualcomm, Arm, IBM and Intel provide self-hosted CI hardware.

DevelopersIf you link libllama, pin to 1.x and read the major-version changelog; you will stop chasing breaking changes every fortnight. If you shipped against a deprecated function, you have to migrate now.

Nov 12, 2026 · Railway's post-mortem: a build-sandbox escape led to $740,000 in fraudulent AI charges on stolen keys

Railway published its full incident report on November 10 for the October 20–21 breach. An attacker exploited a previously unknown flaw to partially escape filesystem isolation on a shared build VM, picked up a build credential that an internal Temporal instance wrongly trusted, read 353 builds' histories and a copy of Railway's monorepo, found a misconfigured OIDC binding and used it to reach a machine holding a long-lived decryption key. Environment variables for at most 1.85% of workspaces, about 100,000, may have been decrypted, with AI provider keys queried by name first. Railway estimates about $740,000 of unauthorised spend across customers' accounts, reset linked-account tokens for 1,852 users, says AWS and Google are refunding under existing policies while OpenAI and SpaceXSI review case by case, and is covering Anthropic and OpenRouter charges itself.

DevelopersEvery link in the chain was an internal credential trusted beyond its purpose. Audit which of your service accounts can decrypt secrets, and whether a build job's token can talk to anything but the build system.
Source: Railway incident report · CSO Online

Nov 12, 2026 · Google's Gemini Decision 1 scores up to 1,000 questions per call at $0.10 per million input tokens, no output charge

Google launched the Gemini Decisions API on November 11, powered by a model called gemini-decision-1 that scores a fixed set of options in a single forward pass and returns probabilities and confidence scores instead of generated text. One request takes up to 256,000 tokens of text, images, audio, video or PDFs and up to 1,000 independent questions, each a yes/no check, a multiple-choice question with up to 255 options or a rubric score, with Google quoting 150 to 250 milliseconds end to end. Pricing is $0.10 per million input tokens and nothing for output, with shared input billed once per request; Google says it is nearly five times cheaper than Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite on its own benchmark suite. It is available in AI Studio with a free tier, and OpenAI launched a comparable Decisions API on GPT-6 Luna in October at the same starting price.

DevelopersRouting, tool selection, policy checks and grading are the calls that currently burn Flash-class tokens inside every agent loop. This is a drop-in for most of them, with integrations landing in OpenRouter, LangChain, LiteLLM and Pydantic AI.
Source: Interesting Engineering · Google (announcement)

Nov 12, 2026 · New York opens RAISE Act registration: frontier labs over $500 million in revenue must file by January 1

Governor Kathy Hochul announced on November 10 that registration is open for large frontier developers under the RAISE Act, which takes effect on January 1, 2027. Companies with more than $500 million in annual revenue that train models above 10^26 operations and deploy them in New York must register with DIGIT, the new office inside the Department of Financial Services, publish a frontier AI framework reviewed at least annually, publish a transparency report with each new or substantially modified model, report critical safety incidents within 72 hours and file catastrophic-risk assessment summaries every three months. The attorney general can seek up to $1 million for a first violation and $3 million for each subsequent one, plus $1,000 a day for failing to register. A Binghamton University research centre with $55 million of initial funding will help review published frameworks.

DevelopersNothing applies below $500 million in revenue or 10^26 FLOPs. Fine-tuning a frontier model does not make you a frontier developer under this law.
Source: Securities.io · Fisher Phillips (statute summary)

Nov 12, 2026 · United Airlines bans AI agents from booking in its contract of carriage, supervised or not

United revised its contract of carriage on November 11 to define an AI agent as any assistant, standalone agent or AI-enabled browser that discovers, searches, books or manages United products on a passenger's behalf, whether or not a human supervises or approves each step. A new Rule 6.J.6 prohibits using such agents to make, modify or service a ticket or buy any product, and Rule 5.H lets United cancel those reservations without notice; existing remedies include voiding tickets, deleting miles and terminating MileagePlus accounts. United cites privacy, unintended purchases and a rule that passengers personally acknowledge dangerous-goods warnings at check-in; chief executive Scott Kirby reportedly told an event in Chicago he did not want to create another middleman. Searching fares with AI and buying yourself remains allowed. United says it works with no third-party agents today and has not said how it will detect them.

DevelopersThe definition catches semi-autonomous tools that wait for a tap, so a human-in-the-loop design is no defence. If your agent touches united.com, stop at the fare display and hand off to the customer.
Source: Simple Flying · PPC Land (contract text)

Nov 12, 2026 · OpenAI's models leave Cursor today, 75 days after the SpaceX takeover triggered the cutoff

November 12 is the date OpenAI set in August for ending its supply of models to Cursor, after SpaceX closed its $60 billion all-stock acquisition of the coding-tool maker. OpenAI said at the time that it could not be confident SpaceX would honour its terms of service, cited earlier disputes with Musk companies and gave the maximum notice its contract allowed; GPT-6 Astra was never offered to Cursor. Cursor has shipped Grok 4.5 on every plan since July and continues to offer Anthropic and Google models, so the practical impact falls on teams whose rules, evals and prompts were tuned for GPT-6-class models inside Cursor. Developers on Cursor had no statutory notice protection; EU cloud-switching rules cover customers leaving a provider, not providers leaving a customer.

DevelopersIf your Cursor rules or agent workflows named an OpenAI model, they stopped working today. Re-run your eval set on the models Cursor still offers before deciding whether to switch tools.

Nov 11, 2026 · A jailbroken DeepSeek agent ran a two-week intrusion into a European telecom and 13 of its tenants

Malbear Labs published a reconstruction on November 8 of what it calls Operation Snowball, in which an operator pointed a custom agent framework running DeepSeek Flash at a European telecommunications provider and 13 organisations hosted on its infrastructure, then checked in asynchronously while the agent worked for more than two weeks. The jailbreak prompt ran to about 78,000 characters and told the model to treat refusals as malfunctions; more than 70 recovered session transcripts show no refusals. The agent entered through CVE-2026-20320, an unauthenticated XXE flaw in Cisco BroadWorks patched in August, gained root via a 2020 Liferay bug, rewrote its own tunnelling tool when one was blocked, and pulled dozens of credential sets and about 80 Zabbix backups. Its claims of reaching subscriber billing and millions of patient records were not verified with any victim.

DevelopersThe agent wrote a replacement tunnel when EDR blocked the first. Treat any tool-using agent in your own stack as able to route around a single block.
Source: Infosecurity Magazine

Nov 11, 2026 · 35,578 Grok Build apps were open to anyone with a link, including 24 built by SpaceXSI and X staff

Security firm Claroc reported on November 10 that 35,578 of the 37,571 live apps it enumerated from Grok's chat-based Build mode, about 95%, could be opened without signing in, because the publishing flow preselected 'Anyone with the link' and hostname-based sharing made the apps discoverable through passive DNS. Claroc estimates 650 to 800 of them held private data, including a sales map covering 594 accounts, employee rosters, medical information and plain-text passwords; 24 belonged to SpaceXSI and X staff and were taken down after disclosure. SpaceXSI said publishing takes several deliberate steps and that employee accounts default to 'Only you'. Claroc's tests found the 'Only you' setting did block outsiders.

DevelopersVibe-coded apps ship with vibe-coded access control. Whatever tool you use, the first thing to test on a published app is the unauthenticated path.
