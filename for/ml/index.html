Nov 9, 2026 · Anthropic reportedly paid $3.4 billion for 1.7 gigawatts of gas generators

Anthropic took over OnePWR Solutions' orders for roughly 1.7 gigawatts of Caterpillar gas-fired generators in a deal signed in late September and worth about $3.4 billion, the Financial Times reported on November 8, citing one person familiar with it. The FT called it the largest known single purchase of power equipment by an AI company; neither party commented. Anthropic's amended IPO filing last week disclosed about $895 billion in commitments, almost all for compute and infrastructure.

ML engineersOn-site gas means training capacity can come online on the lab's timetable rather than the utility's. Expect the compute gap between labs that can self-power and those that cannot to widen.

Nov 9, 2026 · Claude Code's 'don't ask again' button put 2,539 secrets in public GitHub repos

Security firm Apiiro found that when a Claude Code user approves a shell command with 'Yes, and don't ask again', the exact command is saved to .claude/settings.local.json in the project, including any password or token typed into it. Scanning 104,185 public copies of the file, Apiiro found 1,122 containing 1,379 live-looking secrets, including 379 database passwords and 23 AWS keys; with older versions and transcripts the total reached 2,539 secrets across 1,994 repositories. Claude Code head Boris Cherny said on Hacker News that Anthropic is looking into it and that the tool has not historically scrubbed secrets.

ML engineersOf 16 coding agents Apiiro compared, Claude Code was the only one confirmed to persist full approved commands in a project file by default. Agent UX decisions are security decisions.
Source: The Hacker News · iTnews

Nov 9, 2026 · DeepMind says AI agents found two weaknesses in 1024-bit RSA; 2048-bit keys unaffected

Google DeepMind said on November 6 that supervised AI agents produced two working papers describing weaknesses in legacy 1024-bit RSA: a signature-forgery method estimated at roughly 1,600 times more efficient than the prior best when the attacker controls part of the signed data, and a batch-factoring approach about 30 times cheaper per additional key than the multiple number field sieve. One agent generated proofs and a second verified them in Lean 4. The papers are not peer reviewed, the forgery gain is a theoretical estimate, and DeepMind says keys of 2048 bits and above are not affected.

ML engineersThe research pattern, one agent proposing and another formally verifying in Lean, is the same loop that produced recent maths results. Expect it to be pointed at more cryptographic assumptions.
Source: DEV Community (summary of DeepMind's working papers)

Nov 9, 2026 · Thinking Machines ships Inkling 2 Small: 594B-parameter MoE, 17B active, weights promised under Apache 2.0

Thinking Machines Lab released Inkling 2 Small on November 5, a sparse mixture-of-experts model with 594 billion total and 17 billion active parameters, a 1-million-token context window, and image input. It was pretrained on 8.4 trillion tokens in 68 hours on Nvidia GB300 racks using native NVFP4, then trained with reinforcement learning for nine days on 2,048 GPUs. Inference is available through the company's Tinker platform at a listed $0.30 per million input tokens and $1.00 output; full weights are promised under Apache 2.0 within weeks, conditional on external safety testing.

ML engineers17B active parameters out of 594B is an aggressive sparsity ratio, and the 68-hour NVFP4 pretraining run is a data point on how cheap frontier-adjacent pretraining has become. The company says it still trails GPT-6.1 Sol on its own alignment evals.
Source: Thinking Machines Lab · Unite.AI

Nov 9, 2026 · Physical AI goes public: Agility Robotics trades on Nasdaq as Axon puts $1.2 billion into Skydio

Agility Robotics became the first pure-play humanoid company on a US exchange on November 5, trading as AGLT after closing its merger with Churchill Capital Corp XI and raising about $465 million; it reported $174,000 of revenue and a $362 million loss in the first half of 2026 against more than $300 million in committed orders for a Digit 5 robot that ships in 2027. Nov 9, 2026 · Cassidy's PATIENT AI Act draft would add a third-party FDA review path for health AI; comments due December 1

Senate HELP Committee chair Bill Cassidy's discussion draft, released October 29 with stakeholder comments requested by December 1, proposes a voluntary FDA preliminary review for software components of 'integrated devices', an accredited third-party review programme for low- and moderate-risk software devices, pre-submitted change-control plans, and a Medicare coverage pathway for AI services. An analysis by the FDA Law Blog published November 8 argues much of the FDA title duplicates authority the agency already has and could add procedure without shortening time to market.

ML engineersPredetermined change-control plans filed during review would formalise how often a deployed model can be updated. Build your model-update cadence around what regulators will accept, not what your pipeline allows.
Source: FDA Law Blog · AACOM

Nov 8, 2026 · OpenAI launches GPT-6 Cyber and rates it 'Critical' for cybersecurity capability

OpenAI released GPT-6 Cyber on November 5, a version of GPT-6 Astra tuned for authorised vulnerability discovery, exploit development and penetration testing. It is the first model OpenAI has classified as 'Critical' under its Preparedness Framework, one step above the 'High' rating of its predecessor, and is available only to vetted customers in the company's Daybreak Red programme. OpenAI says the model found more than 80 previously unknown vulnerabilities in Firefox during testing.
