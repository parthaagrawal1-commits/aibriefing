Anthropic prices its IPO at $83, above the $70–78 range: 980 million shares, about $81 billion raised before a 147-million-share option, ticker ANT starts trading on Nasdaq today

Anthropic priced 980 million Class A shares at $83 on Wednesday night, 956.4 million of them sold by the company and 23.6 million by existing holders, with a 30-day option for the banks to buy up to 147 million more. The headline raise of roughly $81 billion makes this the largest IPO on record, ahead of SpaceX, and Bloomberg puts the total at $107.3 billion once the private placement from chip and cloud partners is included. The amended prospectus showed third-quarter revenue of about $17 billion, nine-month revenue of $33.2 billion and a $567 million operating loss, against roughly $895 billion of computing commitments. Trading opens today on the Nasdaq Global Select Market; the deal closes Friday.

The one thing to know today

Patch Java today, not this quarter. Java4Shell (CVE-2026-114938) sits in the runtime's accelerated Base64 path on x86-64 machines with AVX-512 VBMI, so every JVM you run, including the ones buried in container images and vendor appliances, is in scope. Upgrade to Oracle's 8u513, 11.0.33.1, 17.0.21.1, 21.0.13.1, 25.0.5.1 or 27.0.1.1 (or your distribution's equivalent), restart every service, and confirm with jcmd <pid> VM.version that the running build actually changed.
