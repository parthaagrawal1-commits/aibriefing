Nvidia's board opens an independent probe after Huang admits an affair with the alleged Megaspeed informant

Nvidia's board said on November 8 that independent directors and outside counsel will review CEO Jensen Huang's relationship with Alice Huang, a Megaspeed executive that a US intelligence memo reportedly alleges was recruited by China's Ministry of State Security. In a message to staff, Huang acknowledged the affair and apologised; the board said it has confidence in him and no reason to believe company or national-security secrets were compromised. House committee chairs have asked for a closed-door briefing on November 18.

The one thing to know today

AI agents are now a line item on both sides of the security ledger. Check Point traced an autonomous ARTEX campaign across 200 organisations in 14 countries, a researcher's agent swarm surfaced a ten-year-old OpenSSH password bypass that was exploited within nine hours of disclosure, and DeepMind's agents found two weaknesses in 1024-bit RSA. The defenders' tools and the attackers' tools are the same tools.
